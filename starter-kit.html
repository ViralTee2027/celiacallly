Important health note

This workbook is for general education and organization only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a food-safety certification. It cannot determine whether a food, product, kitchen, restaurant or meal is suitable for an individual with celiac disease. Food rules and product information can vary by country and change over time. Always check current information and consult your physician or a registered dietitian experienced in celiac disease for personal guidance.
