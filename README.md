# Celiacally

Bilingual English/French responsive static storefront.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. Open repository **Settings** → **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select **main** and **/(root)**, then Save.
4. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish.

Expected URL: https://viraltee2027.github.io/celiacallly/

## Before taking orders
- Replace the placeholder contact email `hello@celiacally.com` with a real business inbox.
- Upload final digital products and add genuine checkout links only after payment setup.
- Have celiac-related safety content reviewed by a qualified registered dietitian experienced in celiac disease.
- Add privacy, refund, and terms pages appropriate to your business and sales locations.

This starter site does not process payments or collect customer data. Listings are marked coming soon until actual downloads and checkout are configured.
