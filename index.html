A little about us

Made for the everyday details.

Celiacally is a digital resource brand focused on the practical side of living with celiac disease. We create clear, welcoming tools to support planning and communication — from the kitchen to the grocery store and beyond.

PracticalUseful pages for everyday routines.
ThoughtfulClear, calm and easy to use.

Important information

Helpful planning, not medical advice.

Celiacally's downloads are organizational and educational resources. They do not replace advice from your physician or a registered dietitian experienced in celiac disease. Food labels, ingredients and manufacturing practices can change and differ by country. Always check current product information and seek qualified guidance when unsure. No planner or checklist can guarantee that a food, product, restaurant or meal is safe.
FAQ

A few helpful answers.

Are the downloads available now?

The first collection is being prepared. Product pages will be updated when reviewed downloads are ready to purchase.

Are the resources medical advice?

No. They are organizational and educational tools, not medical advice or a food-safety certification.

Can I use the planners on a tablet?

The planned products will include printable PDFs and tablet-friendly PDF pages. App-specific interactive features may vary.

Will the resources be bilingual?

Yes. Celiacally is being developed in English and French.
