Why Celiacally exists

A little more clarity for everyday life.

Living with celiac disease can mean keeping track of a lot of details — planning meals, checking information, preparing for time away from home and explaining your needs. Celiacally was created to make the organizing side of that everyday routine feel a little more manageable.

We create thoughtfully designed digital planners, checklists and worksheets for people with celiac disease, as well as the family members and caregivers who support them. Our goal is not to tell you what is safe to eat, but to give you practical ways to keep your own information organized, prepare questions and communicate clearly.

Celiacally is being built in English and French, with a focus on resources that feel welcoming, straightforward and easy to use in real life.

Meet the Founder — Trisha

Celiacally was created by Trisha, a mother whose family’s life changed when her 9-year-old son was diagnosed with celiac disease.

The diagnosis brought new challenges and changes to everyday life, from understanding gluten-free living to navigating food choices and planning ahead. Through Celiacally, Trisha hopes to make that journey a little easier for other families and individuals by creating practical, easy-to-use resources in English and French.

Important information

Helpful planning, not medical advice.

Celiacally's downloads are organizational and educational resources. They do not replace advice from your physician or a registered dietitian experienced in celiac disease. Food labels, ingredients and manufacturing practices can change and differ by country. Always check current product information and seek qualified guidance when unsure. No planner or checklist can guarantee that a food, product, restaurant or meal is safe.
FAQ

Good to know.

A few details about our resources, downloads and the way we approach celiac-friendly planning.

What is Celiacally?

Celiacally creates practical digital resources to help make the everyday tasks of living with celiac disease easier to organize. Our tools focus on planning, preparation and communication.

Who are the resources for?

They are designed for people living with celiac disease and the family members or caregivers who help with everyday planning. You can adapt the pages to your own routines and the guidance you receive from your healthcare team.

Are the products available to buy yet?

Our first collection is in development. Items marked “Coming soon” are not available to purchase yet. We will update each product listing when it is ready and include its price, file format and what is included.

What will I receive when I buy a download?

Products are being developed as digital files, including printable PDFs and selected pages designed for use on a tablet. The product page will explain exactly which files are included and how to access them before you purchase.

Will resources be available in English and French?

Yes. Celiacally is being developed in both English and French. The language options available will be clearly shown on each product page.

Do Celiacally planners tell me which foods or restaurants are safe?

No. Our planners and question prompts help you organize information and communicate your needs, but they do not test, verify or certify foods, brands, kitchens or restaurants. Ingredients and preparation practices can change, so check current information directly with the manufacturer or food provider and follow advice from your qualified healthcare team.

Are these resources medical advice?

No. Celiacally provides educational and organizational materials only. Our resources do not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a physician or a registered dietitian experienced in celiac disease. Please ask your healthcare professional about personal medical or dietary questions.
